# Product Hunt launch copy

Name: Lifecraft Quests

Tagline: Turn real tasks into blocks for your own voxel world

Product link: https://paipeline.github.io/lifecraft-quests/

Repository: https://github.com/paipeline/lifecraft-quests

Pricing: Free

Suggested tags: Productivity, Open Source, Games

Description:

Finish real tasks, collect XP and blocks, and build a small voxel camp. Lifecraft Quests combines a walkable world, a focus timer and a daily calendar with deadlines and free time. Free and open source, with tasks and progress stored in your browser. The current interface is in Chinese.

First comment:

I asked Codex to make me a life calendar. The first version felt too serious, so I asked for something I could play, inspired by my love of Minecraft.

That became Lifecraft Quests. Pick a real task, start a focus session, then come back for XP and building materials when you've finished. You can unlock a farm, library and beacon, or place blocks yourself. The ordinary calendar is still there when you want to plan your time.

I wanted a little world that grows with what I do. There are no missed-day penalties. Tasks and progress stay in the browser, and the source is open so you can run it yourself. It is an early version, currently with a Chinese interface; the screenshots use fictional demo tasks.

I'd like to hear whether building the camp makes it easier to start a task, and where the game gets in the way of actually doing the work.
